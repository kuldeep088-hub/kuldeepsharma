Common things people ask before we work together.

What kind of businesses do you work with?
+
I work best with growth-focused startups, D2C brands, and SaaS products that are ready to invest in systems, not just one-off campaigns. If you want quick hacks, I am probably not your person. If you want real, compounding growth built on AI and smart marketing, let us talk.
How do you approach a new project?
+
Every project starts with understanding where growth is leaking. I audit your traffic, funnel, ad accounts, and existing systems. Then I identify the two or three highest-leverage changes that will drive the most results and start there. No bloated strategies, just prioritized execution.
What makes your SEO approach different?
+
Traditional SEO only covers Google. My approach also includes AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), which gets your brand cited inside AI answers on ChatGPT and Perplexity, and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), which ensures you appear in location-based and geo-targeted results. You get full-spectrum coverage, not just one platform.
How quickly can I expect results?
+
Meta Ads and lead generation systems typically start showing results within 2 to 4 weeks. SEO and AEO take 60 to 90 days for meaningful traction. AI automation workflows deliver time savings the day they go live. I set honest timelines and track everything transparently.
How do we get started?
+
Send me a quick message on WhatsApp or email. We will get on a short call to map out what you need, what is possible, and whether we are a good fit. No pitch decks, no pressure. Just an honest conversation about your growth.
